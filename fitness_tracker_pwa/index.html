How we'll interpret your scale

Eight-electrode BIA scales are useful for repeatable tracking, but body-fat and muscle-mass estimates can fluctuate with hydration.

Don't adjust food because of one week's body-fat number. Compare 4–6-week trends with waist, strength, and recovery.

Best measurement habits

Measure at the same time each week, ideally before exercise. Avoid interpreting changes immediately after heavy training, alcohol, unusually salty food, or a major carbohydrate change.
